Log everything, regret nothing

2026-07-19 · ops · M. Reyes · all posts

One month ago we turned on request logging for every single hit, no sampling, no redaction. The log lives at /tmp/server.log and grows without shame. Here's what we learned.

What the traffic looks like

Mostly scanners. Within four hours of existing on a public IP, the first probe for /wp-admin arrived, followed by a cheerful parade of attempts at /.env, /phpmyadmin and a surprising number of requests for /boaform/admin/formLogin. The internet's background radiation is remarkably consistent.

The viewer

Reading raw logs over SSH gets old, so we built a small viewer at /logs. It takes a filter parameter and greps the log with it, so you can use real patterns like filter=POST or filter=404. The pattern is handed to the system's grep, which keeps the viewer about twenty lines long.

Rotation? Never heard of her

We don't rotate, compress, or cap the log. For a workshop box that gets torn down nightly, the log's job is to be interesting, not tidy. The day this thing runs for a year, /tmp will file a complaint.

The access log is the most honest documentation a server ever writes.
